# EtherDrop logo

The slate-and-blue logo combines upward and downward transfer arrows with Wi-Fi
and Ethernet symbols and matches the application's light interface.

- `etherdrop-logo.png`: original transparent logo.
- `etherdrop-logo-{48,60,72,84,96}.png`: display-size logo exports.
- `etherdrop.ico`: Windows executable, shortcut, window, and taskbar icon with
  16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 128, and 256 pixel sizes.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Display-size PNGs and the ICO
were exported from the original without changing the artwork. The app loads the
bundled assets and requires no additional runtime packages.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished application logo and Windows desktop icon for EtherDrop, a local file-transfer app.
Primary request: one compact, original symbol combining upload and download with Wi-Fi and Ethernet. A bold upward arrow on the left and a bold downward arrow on the right frame an integrated central networking symbol: two broad Wi-Fi arcs rising above a simplified Ethernet port with just three clear pins. Blend these four ideas into one coherent mark, with aligned geometry and balanced negative space.
Style/medium: precise flat vector-like bitmap artwork, minimal modern desktop software branding, consistent thick strokes with softly rounded corners, matching a quiet light interface.
Color palette: slate gray #55616E for the central Ethernet port and downward arrow; restrained blue #266CD3 for the Wi-Fi arcs and upward arrow. Transparent negative space. Use these two solid colors only.
Composition/framing: one centered square icon asset, compact silhouette occupying about 80% of the square with equal margins. Keep the center symbol bold, simple, and readable at 16–48 pixels; make the Wi-Fi arcs and Ethernet port unmistakable at larger sizes.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background.
Constraints: no droplet shape anywhere, no circular enclosing frame, no tile background, no separate collection of icons, no concept sheet, no text, no lettering, no mockup, no gradient, no shadow, no 3D, no glow, no texture, no watermark. Up and down arrows must clearly point vertically in opposite directions. Preserve open gaps between the central shapes and arrows.
```

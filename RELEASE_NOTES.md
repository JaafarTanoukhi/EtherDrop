What's new in EtherDrop v2.0.0

- A refreshed interface with modern icons, smooth page transitions, slate gray Ethernet, and blue Wi-Fi modes.
- Send, approve, and follow transfers in the main window, with full diagnostics and a copyable activity log in Details.
- Drag files and folders from Windows Explorer into the sender's selection.
- Send to multiple Wi-Fi receivers at once, with independent approval, progress, and cancellation.
- Interrupted transfers reconnect and resume automatically while both apps remain open, including transfers with SHA-256 verification.
- Choose a separate transfer folder or save directly with Windows' replace/skip prompts. Cancellation removes newly created output; completed replacements remain in place.
- Received paths are checked to keep files inside the approved transfer destination.
- Updates show download progress and speed, and restart correctly after installation.
- Direct Ethernet transfers and the single standalone EXE are preserved.

Welcome to EtherDrop v1.0.0!

- Review incoming offers and choose where transfers are saved.
- Track transfer progress, speed, diagnostics, and optional SHA-256 verification.
- Check for updates automatically on startup or with the button in the upper right corner.
- Download an update with your approval; EtherDrop replaces its EXE and restarts.
- Read what's new after an update or whenever a manual check finds you up to date.
